# Asset credits

- `models/alder.glb`, `dead_tree.glb`, `bush.glb`, `boulder_a/b/c.glb` — HIGHGROUND's own baked models (this author), reused here.

Everything else on the battlefield — the mage (skeleton, body and every animation, `js/view/mage.js`), the golems, the sky, terrain, grass, crystals, standing stones, walls and effects — is generated in code. No third-party or public-domain models are used.
